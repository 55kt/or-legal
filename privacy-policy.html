Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Or — Decide Instantly ("we", "our", or "the App"), published by Volos Software Development, handles your information. We are committed to collecting as little data as possible and being transparent about it. The App has two modes: an AI mode, and a Social mode where users ask and answer each other's questions.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only what is needed to run the App's two modes.

Questions you ask in AI mode: When you ask the AI, your question text is sent to a third-party AI provider (OpenAI) to generate a response. We do not store these questions on our servers; they are processed in real time and are not linked to your identity.

Content you post in Social mode: When you publish a question, or answer someone else's, the text you write (your question and its two options, or your short answer) is stored on our backend so other users can see and respond to it. This content is visible to other users of the App. It is associated only with a random, anonymous identifier — never with your name or real identity.

Anonymous account: To operate Social mode, the App signs you in anonymously through Firebase Authentication. This creates a random identifier for your device that contains no name, email, or password. We use it to link your questions to your device (so we can notify you when they are answered) and to enforce blocking and moderation.

Notification token: If you allow notifications, we store an anonymous push token (via Firebase Cloud Messaging and the Apple Push Notification service) so we can alert you when your question is answered. It is not linked to any personal information.

Subscription information: If you subscribe to Or Premium, the purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never collect, store, or have access to your payment details or Apple ID.

Usage data: We keep a daily request counter locally on your device to manage the free-tier limit. This counter never leaves your device.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect personally identifying information: no name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, or browsing history. The anonymous account and notification token contain no personal data. We do not ask you to register with personal details, we do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not use advertising SDKs.

3. Community Content & Moderation

Social mode is a space for user-generated content, and we work to keep it safe. Every question and answer is automatically screened against a profanity and abuse blocklist before it appears, and anything that matches is hidden. You can report any question (with a reason) and block any user directly in the App; blocking removes that user's content from your feed, and reporting a question also blocks its author. We act on reports and can remove objectionable content within 24 hours. Questions left unanswered are automatically retired after 24 hours.

4. Third-Party Services

Google Firebase: Social mode, anonymous sign-in, and notifications run on Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore, Firebase Authentication, and Firebase Cloud Messaging). Google processes this data as a service provider under its privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

OpenAI: Questions asked in AI mode are sent to OpenAI to generate a response; only the text of your question is sent: openai.com/privacy.

Apple StoreKit: Subscription purchases are handled by Apple under its privacy policy: apple.com/privacy.

5. Data Storage & Retention

The local request counter stays on your device. Social content, your anonymous identifier, and your notification token are stored on Google Firebase servers. Questions left unanswered are automatically retired after 24 hours. If you delete the App, your local data is permanently removed; content already published to the Social pool may remain, but you can ask us to delete it (see below).

6. Your Choices & Data Deletion

You can turn notifications off at any time in your device Settings. Because Social accounts are anonymous, the simplest way to stop all data collection is to delete the App. To request deletion of content you have posted or of your anonymous record, email us at the address below and we will remove it.

7. Children's Privacy

Or — Decide Instantly is not directed at children under 16, and Social mode requires you to confirm that you are at least 16. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with a new date.

9. Contact Us

Questions about your privacy? Contact us at: volossoftware@icloud.com
